## Featured project · Projeto em destaque

### [QuoteLab · QA automation showcase](https://github.com/LucasBonno/qa-automation-showcase)

[![CI](https://github.com/LucasBonno/qa-automation-showcase/actions/workflows/ci.yml/badge.svg)](https://github.com/LucasBonno/qa-automation-showcase/actions/workflows/ci.yml)

[![Playwright signs in, fills a quote, and the test compares the result with an independent calculation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LucasBonno/qa-automation-showcase/main/docs/img/demo.gif)](https://github.com/LucasBonno/qa-automation-showcase)

🇬🇧 A complete **Playwright + TypeScript** test project, runnable by anyone with `npm install`: **415 tests** through the UI and the API, every amount on screen checked against an **independent calculation** in exact arithmetic, access control by role, an approval workflow, and a **mutation check** that injects 10 deliberate defects and confirms the tests catch all of them. CI runs on every push and the reports are public.

🇧🇷 Um projeto completo de testes com **Playwright + TypeScript**, que qualquer pessoa roda com `npm install`: **415 testes** pela interface e pela API, cada valor da tela conferido contra um **cálculo independente** em aritmética exata, controle de acesso por perfil, fluxo de aprovação e uma **verificação de mutação** que injeta 10 defeitos propositais e confirma que os testes pegam todos. O CI roda a cada envio e os relatórios são públicos.

👉 [Live reports · Relatórios publicados](https://lucasbonno.github.io/qa-automation-showcase/) · [Code · Código](https://github.com/LucasBonno/qa-automation-showcase)

- **Stable tests:** no sleeps, isolated data, clear failures.
- **Showing evidence:** reports, CI, and tests that prove they can fail.
